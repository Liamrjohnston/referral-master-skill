# Credits

Referral Master is written in our own words. No book text is reproduced. These are the people and sources it learned from, and how each was read.

## Books and experts

| Source | What it taught | How we read it |
|---|---|---|
| John Jantsch, *The Referral Engine* (2010) | Be referable before you ask; champions; teach referrers who your ideal client is and the trigger phrases; plant the ask at signing; partners and the "introduction in reverse"; cash usually embarrasses the referrer | Official audiobook sample in his voice, a 2 hour workshop where he teaches the book, his Duct Tape Marketing podcast transcripts and articles |
| Bob Burg, *Endless Referrals* (3rd ed., 2005) | The referral bridge; describe visible signs, not features; never ask "do you know anyone"; shrink their world to small groups they can picture; the owner makes first contact; thank every referral whatever the outcome; no incentives in advance | Official Preface and Chapter 1 excerpt, his networking question sheet, his own Endless Referrals Action Tip videos and posts |
| Alex Hormozi, *$100M Leads* (2023) | Ask "who", not "if"; get a live three-way introduction; budget rewards against the cost of winning a client; two-sided, matched rewards; never leave a client unsure what happens next | His own free reading of the Customer Referrals and Warm Outreach parts on his official podcast feed, and his free Referral Playbook lesson |
| Jay Baer and Daniel Lemin, *Talk Triggers* (2018) | Good work keeps clients but doesn't make them talk; one small, repeatable, relevant surprise | The authors' free official bonus files, keynotes and interviews |
| Michael Port, *Book Yourself Solid* | Only ask happy, ideal clients; the ask has to be earned; ask for an introduction email, not a name or a card; partners with no commissions; update the referrer at about 3 and 6 weeks | His official CreativeLive referral lesson, his talks, and publisher samples of Chapter 1 |
| Fred Reichheld and Bain (Net Promoter) | Promoters, passives and detractors; earned growth | Bain's official Net Promoter pages, a Bain brief and Bain podcasts with Reichheld |
| Robert Cialdini; Jonah Berger | Open with the result; make passing it on make the referrer look good | Cialdini's own site and a Stanford interview; Berger's Wharton paper and interview |

## Rules

- Google's review policy; Competition Bureau Canada on fake and incentivised reviews; US FTC Trade Regulation Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (2024).
