# Worked example

A made-up business, to show the shape and depth of a good card. Every name and number is invented.

## The brief (short version)

Priya is a solo wedding photographer in Winnipeg. Packages run $3,800 to $5,200. She shot 22 weddings this year. 9 came from Instagram, 6 from a wedding planner called Elise, 4 from past couples, 3 from a bridal show. She has never asked a couple for a referral. She wants to post "Refer a friend and get a free print!" on Instagram. She mentions: the Sharmas (married in August) cried at their gallery and have shared it twice; Jordan and Kai (September) are still waiting on their album, which is 3 weeks late; the Lees (2024) sent her two couples and she "never really said thanks"; and the Okoro wedding (June) went fine but they were quiet. She says a booked wedding is worth "maybe $500" to her after costs.

## The card

**The real problem.** **Priya has never asked, and the people who already send her work have never heard back.** The Lees sent 2 couples and were never thanked. Elise sent 6 of her 22 weddings (27%, calc) and is treated like a lucky accident, not a partner. A public "free print" post asks nobody in particular. One correction: at an average package of about $4,500 (estimate, midpoint of $3,800 to $5,200), a wedding is worth far more than $500 in revenue. Fill in your real cost per wedding [N] to get what you keep.

**Who to ask, in order.**

| Who | Group | Why (from the brief) | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Lees | Thank first | Sent 2 couples, never thanked | Today |
| Elise | Partner | Sent 6 of 22 weddings | Call this week |
| The Sharmas | Ask now | Cried at the gallery, shared it twice | Today |
| Jordan and Kai | Fix first | Album 3 weeks late | Ask only after it arrives and they love it |
| The Okoros | Ask later | Fine but quiet | Their first anniversary, with a favourite photo |

**Who to send you.** "A friend who just got engaged and is starting to look at venues."

**Send these.**

To the Lees:
> Hi both, I never properly thanked you for sending me Dana and Sam, and then Meera and Raj. Both weddings were a joy, and they only happened because you put your name behind me. Thank you. A small something is on its way. If you ever hear a friend say they've just got engaged, I'd love to look after them the same way.

To the Sharmas:
> Hi Anjali and Dev, I'm still smiling about how you reacted to your gallery. Thank you for trusting me with your day. Some of my favourite couples came from friends like you, so I might ask: who do you know who's just got engaged? If someone comes to mind, I can send you a two-line note to forward, or you can start a group chat with the three of us. No worries at all if nobody does.

Forwardable intro:
> [Friend], this is Priya, who shot our wedding. She made us both feel relaxed all day and the photos are unreal. Worth a chat if you're looking at venues. [number]

To Elise:
> Elise, thank you for every couple you've sent me this year. I'd love to make it easy for you. When you send someone, I'll get back to them the same day and tell you how it went. Could we grab coffee next week so I can hear what your couples need and send planning leads your way too?

To Jordan and Kai:
> Hi both, your album is 3 weeks late and that's on me. It'll be with you by [date]. I'm sorry for the wait.

Follow-up (once, a week later, if no reply):
> No pressure at all, just bumping this in case it got buried. Thanks either way.

**Rewards and reviews.** Skip the public free print. If Priya wants a thank-you, send it after a referral books, picked for the couple (a framed favourite shot fits her service). Ask every couple from this season for an honest Google review with the direct link, no reward, no star request.

**Do today.**
1. Send the Lees their thank-you.
2. Send the Sharmas the ask.
3. Text Elise to book coffee.
4. Email Jordan and Kai the album date.
5. Put the Okoros' anniversary in the calendar.

**Check these.** Your real cost per wedding. Whether Elise prefers a call or a coffee. That the album date is one you can hit.
